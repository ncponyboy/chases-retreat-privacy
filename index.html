Privacy Policy

Chase's Retreat · Last updated September 2026

Summary

Chase's Retreat is a private app for guests of our vacation rental property in Piney Creek, North Carolina. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app does

The app connects only to a music server located on the property's local network, using a single fixed guest account built into the app. There is no account creation, no sign-up, and no user-entered personal data of any kind.

What we don't do

We don't use analytics, tracking, or advertising services. We don't collect device identifiers, location data, or usage statistics. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere except the property's own local server.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to chasessoftware@gmail.com.
